SIMPLIFIED PROJECT CONCLUSION
=============================

The experiment used 270 real PostgreSQL response-time measurements:
3 table sizes x 3 methods x 30 matched searches.

At 500,000 rows, No index averaged 71.07 ms.
At 500,000 rows, B+ tree averaged 1.77 ms.
At 500,000 rows, Hash averaged 1.67 ms.
B+ tree was approximately 40.21 times faster than No index.
Hash was approximately 42.55 times faster than No index.

No index versus B+ tree paired-test p-value: 1.013e-18
No index versus Hash paired-test p-value: 1.102e-18
B+ tree versus Hash paired-test p-value: 0.3113

Both indexed methods were significantly faster than No index under the tested conditions. Hash had a slightly lower observed mean than B+ tree at 500,000 rows, but their difference was not statistically significant.

These conclusions apply to equality searches on this PostgreSQL database and this computer. Individual timings can change because of caching and background system activity.
